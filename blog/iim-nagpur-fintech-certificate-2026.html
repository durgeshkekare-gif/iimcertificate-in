IIM Nagpur FinTech Certificate — Overview

The PG Certificate in Financial Technologies (FinTech) by IIM Nagpur is a 6-month programme covering the intersection of finance and technology — digital payments, blockchain, lending tech, insurtech, wealthtech, and regulatory technology (RegTech). ~₹1.75L + GST, online with IIM Nagpur campus visit. IIM Nagpur is AACSB accredited, NIRF #25 Management. Gives IIM Nagpur alumni status.

What the FinTech Certificate Covers

  • Digital payments ecosystem — UPI, NPCI, CBDC, payment gateways
  • Lending technology — P2P lending, BNPL, digital credit underwriting
  • Blockchain and DeFi — distributed ledger technology, smart contracts, crypto regulation in India
  • InsurTech — digital insurance, usage-based models, AI in underwriting
  • WealthTech — robo-advisory, digital wealth management, account aggregator framework
  • RegTech — regulatory compliance automation, RBI sandbox, SEBI fintech regulations

Who Is This For?

Best suited for: BFSI professionals who need to understand FinTech disruption; technology professionals wanting to move into financial services; product managers at banks, NBFCs or fintech startups; and investors or consultants who need to evaluate FinTech business models with IIM credentialing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IIM Nagpur FinTech certificate worth it?

Yes for BFSI professionals and tech professionals targeting fintech roles. At ~₹1.75L for 6 months, it gives IIM Nagpur alumni status alongside a curriculum covering India's fintech ecosystem — UPI, digital lending, blockchain, insurtech and regtech. The fee-to-credential ratio is strong.

What is the scope of FinTech in India 2026?

India's fintech market is one of the world's fastest growing — driven by UPI (12B+ monthly transactions), BNPL growth, digital lending, and the Account Aggregator framework. Career opportunities in product management, business development, risk, compliance and analytics at fintech companies and banks offer ₹12-35L CTC for experienced professionals.

Can non-finance professionals do IIM Nagpur FinTech certificate?

Yes — the programme is open to both finance and technology professionals. It bridges both worlds. For tech professionals wanting to move into financial services: IIM Nagpur FinTech gives business understanding of finance + IIM institutional credibility.
