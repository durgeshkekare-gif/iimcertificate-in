IIM Kozhikode NAMP Review 2026 — Is It Right for Senior Professionals?

August 04, 2026 · 6 min read · iimcertificate.in

What IIM Kozhikode NAMP Is — And What It Isn't

NAMP is not a general management programme for all experience levels. It is specifically architected for professionals with 10+ years of experience who already have operational expertise and want strategic leadership frameworks. IIM Kozhikode is ranked #3 by NIRF (Management) — EQUIS and AMBA accredited.

What the Curriculum Covers

  • Module 1: Organisation Functional Competency (market economics, finance, operations, brand)
  • Module 2: Managing Organisations (strategy, M&A, portfolio management, turnaround)
  • Module 3: Executive Presence & Influence (negotiations, EQ, stakeholder management)
  • Module 4: Team Dynamics (leadership, decision-making, change management)
  • Module 5: Digital Business Transformation (AI/ML for decisions, digital strategy)

Honest Assessment — What Works and What to Expect

What works: NAMP's peer cohort is genuinely senior — 10+ year requirement means your classmates have serious careers. The curriculum assumes your operational competence and builds strategic thinking on top. IIM-K's EQUIS + AMBA accreditation carries international recognition.

Be realistic about: NAMP is a certificate, not a degree. At ₹4L + GST (~₹4.72L all-in), it's meaningful value for an IIM-K credential. But go in clear-eyed: this gives you a certificate and alumni status, not a degree and not placement support.

"NAMP is the right choice for the 10-15 year professional who has outgrown general management courses and needs a programme that starts from where they actually are."

Frequently Asked Questions

What is IIM Kozhikode NAMP?

NAMP — New Age Management Programme for Senior Professionals — is IIM Kozhikode's executive certificate designed exclusively for professionals with 10+ years of work experience. 10 months, ₹4,00,000 + GST, online with IIM-K campus immersion. Gives IIM Kozhikode alumni status.

What is the fee for IIM Kozhikode NAMP 2026?

Total programme fee is ₹4,00,000 + GST. Payment in 3 instalments: Booking ₹1,35,000 + Instalment 1 ₹1,35,000 + Instalment 2 ₹1,30,000. Application fee ₹2,000 + GST.

Is 10 years experience mandatory for IIM Kozhikode NAMP?

Yes — the eligibility requirement is a graduate with minimum 10 years of post-qualification work experience. This is a genuine requirement built into the curriculum design, not a loose guideline. The programme assumes operational expertise and focuses on strategic leadership.
