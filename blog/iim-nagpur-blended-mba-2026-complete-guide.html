IIM Nagpur Blended MBA — What Makes It Unique

The IIM Nagpur Blended MBA for Working Professionals is the only MBA degree in India from an IIM institution that a working professional can pursue without quitting their job. Every other IIM offers either: a full-time on-campus MBA (requires 2-year leave), or executive certificate programmes (not a degree). IIM Nagpur is the only IIM that gives you a full UGC-approved MBA degree in blended format.

The WHY — Three Reasons Professionals Choose This

Reason 1: MBA degree, not certificate. IIM executive programmes from IIM-A, IIM-K etc. give certificates. IIM Nagpur gives a degree. For roles where MBA degree is an eligibility criteria, this is the only IIM option for working professionals.

Reason 2: IIM institutional credentials. IIM Nagpur is a government IIM, AACSB accredited, NIRF ranked. The MBA carries IIM Nagpur alumni status — permanent. The institutional weight is categorically different from private or deemed universities.

Reason 3: No job interruption. Weekend online classes + 4 campus immersions per year means zero income disruption. The ₹12.10L fee investment is funded from your salary rather than wiping out savings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fee for IIM Nagpur Blended MBA 2026?

IIM Nagpur Blended MBA 2026 (Batch 2) fee is ₹12,10,000 excluding GST. Application fee is ₹2,500. Payment is in 4 instalments over 2 years. Education loans are available from HDFC, SBI and Axis Bank.

Is IIM Nagpur Blended MBA a real MBA degree?

Yes — it is a full MBA degree awarded by IIM Nagpur, a central government IIM under the Ministry of Education. UGC-DEB approved, AACSB accredited. Graduates receive the same MBA credential as the flagship full-time residential batch.

What is the eligibility for IIM Nagpur Blended MBA?

Graduate with minimum 50% marks from a recognised university AND minimum 3 years of post-qualification work experience. A valid IIM Nagpur entrance test score (IIMNAT) is required — held in July 2026.
