IIM Trichy Banking & Finance Certificate — Overview

The PG Certificate in Banking & Finance by IIM Tiruchirappalli is a 6-month executive programme for BFSI sector professionals. Fee ₹2,41,000 + GST. Delivered online with campus visit at IIM Trichy. IIM Trichy is a central government IIM — AMBA accredited, AACSB member, NIRF #16.

Who Is This For?

The IIM Trichy Banking & Finance programme is specifically designed for professionals in banking, NBFC, insurance, capital markets and fintech who want IIM credentials without the 1-year commitment of the Senior Management Programme. Ideal for Branch Managers, Relationship Managers, Credit Officers, Treasury professionals and FinTech product managers targeting senior roles.

Curriculum — What You Learn

  • Banking operations — credit analysis, NPA management, regulatory compliance
  • Financial markets — equity, debt, derivatives, portfolio management
  • Risk management — credit risk, market risk, operational risk frameworks
  • Corporate finance — capital structure, M&A, valuation for banking professionals
  • FinTech and digital banking — payment systems, UPI, CBDC, lending tech
  • Regulatory framework — RBI guidelines, SEBI regulations, IRDAI for insurance

Frequently Asked Questions

What is IIM Trichy Banking & Finance certificate?

PG Certificate in Banking & Finance by IIM Trichy — a 6-month government IIM programme for BFSI professionals. Fee ₹2.41L + GST. Covers banking operations, financial markets, risk management, corporate finance and FinTech. Gives IIM Trichy alumni status.

Is IIM Trichy a good IIM for finance?

Yes — IIM Trichy (IIM Tiruchirappalli) is a central government IIM under the Ministry of Education. It is AMBA accredited and AACSB member. The Banking & Finance programme is one of the most specifically targeted BFSI programmes among all IIM executive certificates.

What is the eligibility for IIM Trichy Banking & Finance certificate?

Graduate degree with work experience in banking, financial services or insurance sector. Specific minimum experience requirement should be verified on the official programme page.
