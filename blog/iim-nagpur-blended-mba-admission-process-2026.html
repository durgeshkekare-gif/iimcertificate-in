August 11, 2026 · 7 min read · iimcertificate.in

IIM Nagpur Blended MBA Admission — Overview

The IIM Nagpur Blended MBA admission process for Batch 2 (2026-28) has 5 stages: application, IIMNAT entrance test, Group Discussion (GD), Personal Interview (PI), and final selection. The entire process is designed for working professionals — GD/PI rounds are structured to be accessible remotely or over a short campus visit.

Step-by-Step Admission Process

Step 1 — Online Application

  • Apply via official Jaro Education portal or IIM Nagpur's programme page
  • Fill application form: personal details, educational background, work experience
  • Pay application fee: ₹2,500 + GST
  • Upload documents: degree certificate, marksheets, experience letters, ID proof, photograph

Step 2 — IIMNAT Entrance Test

  • Online aptitude test conducted by IIM Nagpur
  • Sections: Verbal Ability, Quantitative Aptitude, Data Interpretation, General Awareness
  • Approximate duration: 2 hours
  • Conducted at designated test centres or online (verify format for Batch 2)
  • No negative marking typically — attempt all questions

Step 3 — Group Discussion (GD)

  • GD on a current business, management, or social topic
  • Evaluates: communication clarity, ability to listen, leadership in group setting, structured thinking
  • Typically 8-10 candidates per GD group
  • Duration: 15-20 minutes discussion

Step 4 — Personal Interview (PI)

  • Interview by IIM Nagpur faculty panel
  • Topics: career journey, professional achievements, why IIM Nagpur, future goals
  • Academic background questions for candidates with weak academics
  • PI weight is significant — preparation on your career story is essential

Step 5 — Selection & Offer

  • Selection criteria: IIMNAT score (30-40%) + GD performance (20%) + PI performance (30-40%) + work experience quality (assessed holistically)
  • Merit list published — top candidates receive offer letters
  • Offer acceptance requires ₹1,00,000 deposit payment

Documents Required for Application

  • Class 10 marksheet and certificate
  • Class 12 marksheet and certificate
  • Graduation degree certificate and all semester marksheets
  • Work experience letters (from each employer — minimum 3 years total)
  • ID proof: Aadhaar / Passport
  • 2 passport-size photographs
  • Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC if applicable)

Frequently Asked Questions

When does IIM Nagpur Blended MBA application open for 2026?

Batch 2 (2026-28) applications opened on 20 May 2026. The IIMNAT entrance test is scheduled for 19 July 2026. Apply early — seats are limited and early applicants have more preparation time for IIMNAT.

Is GD mandatory for IIM Nagpur Blended MBA admission?

Yes — Group Discussion (GD) is part of the admission process after IIMNAT. Candidates who clear the IIMNAT cut-off are called for GD/PI. Preparation tip: practise articulating structured opinions on current business and management topics in 2-3 minutes.

What is the selection weightage for IIM Nagpur Blended MBA?

Approximate weightage: IIMNAT score (30-40%), Personal Interview (30-40%), Group Discussion (15-20%), Work Experience quality (10-15%). The exact weightage may vary by batch — IIM Nagpur evaluates candidates holistically.
