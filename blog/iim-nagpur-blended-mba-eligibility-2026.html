August 11, 2026 · 6 min read · iimcertificate.in

IIM Nagpur Blended MBA — Eligibility at a Glance

Eligibility: Graduate with minimum 50% marks + minimum 3 years post-qualification work experience + valid IIMNAT score (IIM Nagpur's entrance test)

Detailed Eligibility Criteria

CriterionRequirement
Educational qualificationBachelor's degree from any recognised university
Minimum marks50% aggregate (45% for SC/ST candidates)
Work experienceMinimum 3 years of post-qualification full-time work experience
AgeNo upper age limit — programme is open to working professionals of all ages
Entrance testIIMNAT (IIM Nagpur Aptitude Test) — mandatory
StreamAny stream — Engineering, Commerce, Arts, Science, Law, Medicine all eligible
Final year studentsNot eligible — degree must be completed

Work Experience — What Counts?

  • Full-time employment — salaried or self-employed
  • Entrepreneurship with verifiable business operations
  • Freelancing with documentary evidence (contracts, invoices, IT returns)
  • Internships and traineeships generally do NOT count toward the 3-year minimum
  • Part-time work is typically not counted — verify at time of application

IIMNAT — The Entrance Test Explained

IIMNAT (IIM Nagpur Aptitude Test) is IIM Nagpur's own entrance test for the Blended MBA programme. Unlike CAT, IIMNAT is specifically designed for working professionals and is conducted by IIM Nagpur. No CAT score is required for the Blended MBA — IIMNAT is the only entrance requirement.

Who Is Most Likely to Be Selected?

IIM Nagpur evaluates candidates holistically — IIMNAT score, academic background, quality and diversity of work experience, and the personal interview (PI). Candidates with 3-8 years of progressive career experience and strong communication skills tend to be the strongest profiles. Industry diversity within the batch is actively sought — IT, BFSI, manufacturing, healthcare, consulting candidates all have good representation.

Can I Apply With an Arts or Humanities Background?

Yes — the IIM Nagpur Blended MBA has no stream restriction. Graduates from Arts, Humanities, Social Sciences, Law, Medicine, and any other discipline are eligible as long as they meet the 50% marks and 3 years experience requirements. The programme values diverse backgrounds — non-engineering and non-commerce graduates bring perspectives the cohort benefits from.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 3 years experience mandatory for IIM Nagpur Blended MBA?

Yes — the IIM Nagpur Blended MBA for Working Professionals requires a minimum of 3 years of post-qualification full-time work experience. This is a firm eligibility criterion, not a guideline. Candidates who do not meet this requirement at time of application are not considered.

Is IIMNAT difficult to clear?

IIMNAT is designed for working professionals and covers standard aptitude topics: verbal ability, quantitative aptitude, data interpretation, and general awareness. The difficulty level is moderate — comparable to GMAT verbal and quant sections. Candidates with 3+ years of professional experience who brush up on aptitude basics and practice mock tests have strong success rates.

Can I apply to IIM Nagpur Blended MBA with a low graduation percentage?

The minimum is 50% aggregate (45% for SC/ST). Below 50%: you are not eligible for the standard batch. If you scored below 50% in graduation: verify if any relaxation applies to your category. The entrance test and interview performance can compensate somewhat but the graduation percentage is a hard cut-off for eligibility.
